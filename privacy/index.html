Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

Xcreen does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Cleaning preferences stay on your device. The free version uses non-personalized advertising, and an optional lifetime purchase is handled through the app store and RevenueCat.

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Xcreen ("Xcreen," "we," "us," or "our") and the service providers built into the app handle information. It applies to the Xcreen mobile application.

Information stored on your device

Xcreen saves your selected cleaning color, exit-control corner, auto-start choice, and whether certain introductory messages have been shown. These preferences are stored locally on your device so the app can remember your setup. We do not receive them on a server. Removing the app generally removes these local preferences.

Advertising

The free version of Xcreen uses Google AdMob to display a banner on the Home screen. Xcreen currently requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. No advertisement is displayed in cleaning mode, and Pro users are not shown advertisements.

Google and participating ad providers may automatically process information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and improve ads. This may include your IP address (which can indicate general location), app and device information, app- or developer-scoped device identifiers, advertisements shown, interactions with advertisements, performance information, crash or diagnostic information, and your advertising-consent choices. Xcreen does not request access to your device's precise location.

Where required, Xcreen presents Google's consent interface before requesting ads. You can decline consent or manage individual choices. If privacy options are required for your region, they remain available from Settings & Help in the app. Depending on your choices and applicable law, Google may serve non-personalized or limited ads, or no ad may be available.

Purchases and Xcreen Pro

Xcreen Pro is an optional lifetime in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat helps Xcreen validate the purchase, restore access, and determine whether Pro is active. Xcreen does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

RevenueCat creates a random anonymous app-user identifier and may process purchase or receipt information, entitlement status, the time the app was last used, and technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and IP-derived general location. We do not provide RevenueCat with your name or email address, and Xcreen does not offer user accounts.

How information is used

Information described in this policy is used to:

  • remember your cleaning preferences on your device;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Xcreen;
  • show and measure non-personalized ads for free users;
  • record consent choices and comply with privacy requirements;
  • process, validate, and restore Xcreen Pro purchases; and
  • prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers and disclosure

Xcreen relies on Google AdMob for advertising and consent management, RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management, and Apple for app distribution and in-app payment processing. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices and may use subprocessors to provide their services. To the extent they handle user information for Xcreen, we require them to provide the same or equivalent protection described in this policy and required by applicable privacy standards. Xcreen does not independently sell your personal information.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce applicable terms, protect users or the public, or complete a business transfer involving Xcreen, subject to appropriate protections.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain until you change them, reset them, or remove the app. Google, RevenueCat, and Apple retain information according to their policies and as necessary to provide advertising, purchase restoration, security, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal compliance. App-store purchase records may need to be retained even after other information is deleted.

You can change applicable advertising-consent choices through Xcreen's Settings & Help screen. You may also contact us to ask about, correct, or delete information under our control. Because Xcreen does not create user accounts and uses anonymous purchase identifiers, we may need information from your device or a purchase record to locate a relevant record, and in some cases we may not be able to associate a record with you.

Children's privacy

Xcreen is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Xcreen, contact us so we can review the issue.

International processing and security

Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. We use established providers and reasonable safeguards intended to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Xcreen's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version. Material changes will be presented through the app or another appropriate notice when required.
